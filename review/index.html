Which label sits on the papyrus surface?

  • Each item is a close-up slice through a CT scan of a rolled scroll. Lighter grey is papyrus, darker is the gap between wraps. Each voxel is one square.
  • A and B show the same slice with two candidate labels ( yellow band) for one face of a sheet. They come from different methods, and which one appears as A or B is random. A few items are built-in checks with a known answer.
  • Pick the band that follows the sheet's face more exactly: the edge where papyrus meets the gap. A band floating out in the gap or buried inside the sheet is worse. A thin bright or dark fringe right beside a surface is a scanner artifact, not papyrus.
  • Choose About the same when neither is clearly better, and Can't tell when the image doesn't let you judge. Go with your first impression; most items take a few seconds.

Here B is worse: its band floats about 3 voxels out in the dark gap, while A sits on the sheet's face. So this one is “A is better”.
